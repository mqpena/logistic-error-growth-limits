# Phase C mathematical specification and interpretation

The audit uses fixed rounded-radius shells 10 through 20. For each archived field time and member, define `S_k=Et_k+Ef_k`, `a_k=epsilon_k/S_k`, and `w_k=S_k/sum(S_k)`. Direct difference energy supplies epsilon. No shell is dropped when its amplitude approaches an endpoint: invalid denominators invalidate the whole row. The exact band coordinate is `A=sum(w_k*a_k)=sum(epsilon_k)/sum(S_k)`. A fixed-support truth-energy-weighted correlation proxy is reported separately; it is not the historical changing-mask analysis.

The primary sampled derivative is the unsmoothed centered secant on three equal-cadence points; a span-two secant on five equal-cadence points is the prescribed sensitivity. Endpoints and cadence changes are excluded. Both summarize the dense record through time 12. The span-two secant is not a fourth-order derivative. These operators describe finite samples, and their errors are not assumed to be negligible.

For the same sampled derivative operator `D_t`, the exact sampled bookkeeping is

`D_t A = sum(w_k D_t a_k) + sum(D_t w_k (a_k-A)) + R_product`,

where `R_product` is defined by the displayed difference and is reported rather than forced to zero. A derivative product rule is exact in continuous time under suitable regularity; it is generally inexact for centered differences. Tight arithmetic tolerance applies to the finite-sum variance/covariance identity alone, never to `R_product` or to physical closure quality.

The archived independent tangent rate is the mean primary-band energy-growth rate after the inherited configuration burn-in. It defines `D_observed=D_t A-lambda0*A*(1-A)`. No independent shellwise tangent rates or closure operator are supplied by this archive. Consequently, decomposition into physically independent shell clocks and shell-model residuals is unavailable.

An explicitly descriptive decomposition infers `lambda_k=(D_t a_k)/(a_k*(1-a_k))` only at rows where every fixed shell lies in [0.15,0.85]. At those rows, the residual of the shell surrogate is zero by definition. The terms are clock drift `(mean(lambda)-lambda0)*A*(1-A)`, shape `-mean(lambda)*Var(a)`, covariance `Cov(lambda,a*(1-a))`, weight evolution, and the sampled product-rule residual. Their sum reconstructs `D_observed` as arithmetic; it does not independently identify a physical mechanism. Reports show each signed term, the sum, sum of absolute magnitudes, cancellation fraction `1-abs(sum)/sumabs`, and the frequency of opposing signs above a scale-based arithmetic floor. Cancellation involving the discretization residual is distinguished from cancellation among inferred amplitude terms.

The algebraic absolute tolerance is `1e-12*characteristic`, where `characteristic=max(RMS(D_t A), abs(lambda0)*RMS(A*(1-A)), machine_tiny)`; relative tolerance is `1e-10` times summed absolute reconstruction terms. This has the units of a tendency. The sign floor is an arithmetic criterion, not a scientific significance threshold.

A certified continuous-SQG-trajectory bound cannot be evaluated from these snapshots because a continuous derivative/interpolation error envelope is missing. A predictive closure bound additionally lacks independent shell clocks and their residuals. Those unavailability statements remain explicit even if the auxiliary construction below succeeds.

For the auxiliary construction, define a new, fully specified piecewise-linear path `A_PL` between consecutive 0.1-time-unit field samples through time 12. Only contiguous intervals with endpoints in [0,1] are eligible; no late sparse gap is bridged. On each interval its derivative is the exact linear slope, so `h_PL=A_PL_prime-lambda0*A_PL*(1-A_PL)` is a known quadratic polynomial. Start the logistic reference at the first observed amplitude of each contiguous interval; do not align at half height. The Gronwall integral is computed recursively with the exponential kernel. Split intervals at real quadratic sign-change roots, then compare 16- and 32-point Gauss-Legendre evaluations. Their propagated difference estimates quadrature convergence; it is not a rigorous error enclosure. Compare the resulting bound with reference error at sampled times, label the entire result as applying only to the interpolated path, and report loose or overflowing bounds.

The primary band contains no directly forced modes. A continuous quotient for a directly white-noise-forced band can require an Ito correction; this primary-band classical derivative analysis is not extended to those bands. Phase B addresses their sampled physical balances separately.

Inputs are processed one truth group at a time. Source and verified moment-cache hashes and the phase-A gate are prerequisites. No new dynamics, tangent integrations, fits, null tests, bootstrap replications, or model-selection thresholds are introduced. Group summaries preserve the two members within each truth; descriptive leave-one-truth-out summaries are reported when more than one truth is present.

A separate, more informative residual uses the independent band rate as a prescribed common shell reference: `q_ref,k=D_t a_k-lambda0*a_k*(1-a_k)`. Its weighted identity is `D_observed=-lambda0*Var(a)+H+sum(w*q_ref)+R_product`. This residual is available without independently measured shell clocks and does not vanish by definition. It quantifies sampled inadequacy of the explicitly stated common-reference hypothesis, while retaining derivative and storage errors and making no physical-cause attribution. Its termwise cancellation, per-shell residuals, and weighted residual RMS are reported separately from the inferred-rate construction.

The additional snapshot-operator decomposition uses reconstructed shell productions and deterministic modal normalizer tendencies in the unforced primary band. Set `da_op=(Psum-a*Sdot)/S`, `dw_op=(Sdot-w*Sdot_band)/S_band`, `H_op=sum(dw_op*(a-A))`, and `q_ref_op=da_op-lambda0*a*(1-a)`. Independently form `D_op=sum(Psum)/S_band-A*Sdot_band/S_band-lambda0*A*(1-A)` and verify its finite-sum decomposition into `-lambda0*Var(a)+H_op+sum(w*q_ref_op)`. This is an operator evaluation at archived states, not numerical integration, and does not need a fitted shell rate. Dense-time and all125-record summaries are separate; operator-minus-FD differences use common dense stencil support. Moment storage precision and intersnapshot uncertainty remain distinct from arithmetic identity residuals. The continuous physical-trajectory bound remains unavailable.

The execution prerequisite uses the explicitly recorded primary-scope dependency gate in `audit_dependencies.primary_scope`. Phase A reconstruction, source/cache hashes, and primary-scope prerequisites must pass. An archived allocated-increment discrepancy confined to a nonprimary band does not silently discard that source or convert its overall status to PASS: the C record retains `all_band_implementation_checks_passed`, the failed nonprimary discrete checks, and the gate reason. This post-failure dependency clarification changes no numerical formula or tolerance and does not validate the full numerical trajectory. Earlier N256 pilot records retain their original code version.
