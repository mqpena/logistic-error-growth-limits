The operator audit originally summarized all 121 states through time 12, including later decorrelated fluctuations and amplitudes above one. To compare it fairly with the original selected lifecycle, this supplementary table uses the already specified phase-A/B band correlation window `0.15 < rho_b < 0.85`, evaluated at the matching archived field times through time 12. It uses the same fixed shells 10 through 20. No new dynamics, raw-field reconstruction, fitting, resampling, or scientific acceptance threshold is introduced. Frozen dense/all-record results remain unchanged.

Each numerator and its characteristic denominator are recomputed on the matched window. The characteristic is the larger of RMS(operator normalized tendency) and RMS(lambda_b*A*(1-A)) on that window. Operator-versus-finite-difference comparisons use the common valid stencil subset and its own shared operator-based characteristic. Normalization and interband-transfer ratios divide by the RMS signed net normalized production over the matched window. The shell residual RMS is distinguished from RMS of the signed weighted-mean residual.

Per-member sample counts are retained. Two members are averaged within each truth and the median is then taken across all four truths per regime; missing member quantities make the corresponding grouped statistic unavailable, rather than silently reducing the sample. These are descriptive comparisons, not new confirmatory evidence or a continuous-trajectory bound.
